Music popularity depends on many factors including audio features, artist popularity, lyrics, and audience engagement.

Tempo is the speed of a song measured in beats per minute (BPM). Faster songs often create excitement, while slower songs create emotional feelings.

Energy measures how active and intense a song sounds. High-energy songs are commonly used in concerts, festivals, and dance events.

Loudness represents the average volume of a song and is measured in decibels (dB). Proper loudness improves the listening experience.

Song duration can influence listener engagement. Songs between three and five minutes are common in popular music.

Artists who release songs consistently often maintain audience interest over time.

YouTube views are commonly used as an indicator of song popularity, although marketing and social media also influence success.

Machine learning can identify relationships between audio features and music popularity.

Features such as tempo, loudness, energy, and duration are widely used in music analysis.

Music recommendation systems often compare audio features to recommend similar songs.
